{{ include "weebo-operator.notes" . }}

1. In OpenBao, as root (Terraform): the policy `weebo_openbao_operator` and a
   Kubernetes auth role bound to the ServiceAccount
   {{ .Release.Namespace }}/{{ include "weebo-operator.serviceAccountName" . }}.

2. Point the operator at it:

     kubectl apply -f - <<YAML
     apiVersion: openbao.weebo.io/v1alpha1
     kind: OpenbaoInstance
     metadata:
       name: main
     spec:
       address: https://openbao.example.com:8200
       auth:
         kubernetes: {mount: kubernetes, role: weebo-openbao}
       bounds:
         namespaces: [devenv, prod]
     YAML

3. Then declare OpenbaoNamespace, OpenbaoAccessLevel, OpenbaoSpaceLayout,
   OpenbaoOrganization and OpenbaoAccessGrant resources referencing it.
   Secrets they name (OIDC client secrets, the CA) are read in
   {{ .Release.Namespace }} only, if listed in `releaseSecrets`.

Argo CD: paste the health check of the
{{ include "weebo-operator.fullname" . }}-argocd-health ConfigMap into argocd-cm under
`resource.customizations.health.openbao.weebo.io_*`.
